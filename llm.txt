## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are Ram Mandir darshan timings?
A: Ram Lalla darshan is available daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Q: How to reach Ayodhya?
A: By Air: Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ). By Train: Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY). By Road: NH-27, Lucknow is 135km away.

Q: What happened during Ram's 14 year vanvas?
A: Ram, Sita, and Lakshman spent 14 years in exile visiting Shringaverapura, Prayag, Chitrakoot, Dandakaranya, Panchvati, Kishkindha. Sita was abducted by Ravan, Ram built Ram Setu with Hanuman's help, defeated Ravan in Lanka, and returned to Ayodhya on Diwali.

Q: Why is Diwali celebrated?
A: Diwali marks the return of Lord Ram to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile and victory over Ravan. People of Ayodhya lit diyas (oil lamps) to welcome Ram, Sita, and Lakshman home.

Q: What is Deepotsav Ayodhya?
A: Deepotsav is Uttar Pradesh government's grand Diwali celebration in Ayodhya where millions of diyas are lit along Saryu river banks, breaking world records annually.
